Puerto Princesa Underground River

Explore the UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its spectacular limestone karst landscape and subterranean river.

Famous for its stunning limestone cliffs, crystal-clear waters, and vibrant marine life—perfect for island hopping and diving.

A UNESCO Marine Heritage Site and a paradise for divers, known for its diverse coral reefs and abundant marine species.
