Sort These Out Before You Go

Four things that make the trip smoother.

Getting There

Fly into Puerto Princesa, Busuanga (Coron) or El Nido, then continue by van or boat.

Pack for Sun and Sea

Bring reef-safe sunscreen, a dry bag, water shoes and a hat.

Bring cash

ATMs are limited outside town centers. Carry pesos for entrance and eco fees.

Protect the Reefs

Do not touch corals or feed fish, and take all your trash with you.

When to go

Nov to Feb: Cool and Dry

Great for beaches and island hopping. This is the busiest time of year.

Mar to May: Hot and Clear

The calmest, clearest seas. Ideal for diving and lagoon tours.

Jun to Oct: Rainy Season

Fewer crowds and lower prices, but boat trips may be canceled in rough weather.
