What Is League of Legends?

League of Legends (LoL) is a multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game developed by Riot Games and released in 2009. It is free to play on PC and is one of the world's most popular esports, with professional and amateur leagues competing worldwide. The game is rated "T for Teen."

It is a team-based strategy game where two teams of five powerful champions face off to destroy each other's base. Choose from more than 160 champions to make epic plays, secure kills and take down towers as you battle your way to victory.

How a Match Works

Two teams of five players battle to destroy the opposing team's base, known as the Nexus. Here is how the game is laid out.

The Map

Summoner's Rift is split into two sides, Red and Blue. Each side has its own Nexus with two attack towers for additional defense.

The Lanes

Three lanes, Top, Mid and Bottom, connect the two sides, with more attack towers along the way. Each Nexus spawns minions that march toward the enemy and attack anything in range.

The Fountain

Each player spawns in their team's Fountain, a protected area behind the Nexus. Return at any time to buy items, replenish resources and respawn after being eliminated.

Your Champion

Each player pilots a unique champion, gaining experience points to level up and earning gold to buy items that enhance their abilities.

Strategy

Teams plan before the game and adapt as the match goes on, pushing through the lanes and taking down towers to reach the enemy Nexus.

Victory

The first team to destroy their opponent's Nexus wins the game.

How to Download League of Legends

League of Legends is free to play. Follow these four steps to get started.

Download League of Legends
  1. Check that your computer meets Riot's system requirements and has enough free storage for the installer.
  2. Select the download link and run the installer.
  3. Once it is installed, launch the client from the desktop shortcut or by searching your computer for League of Legends.
  4. Sign in with your Riot Games account and set up your in-game account.
