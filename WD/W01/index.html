What's Inside the Realme C33

The full specification sheet, from the processor to the ports.

Network
GSM / HSPA / LTE (4G only, no 5G)
Launch
Announced September 6, 2022. Released September 12, 2022.
Body
164.2 × 75.7 × 8.3 mm, 187 g. Glass front, plastic frame and back. Dual SIM (Nano-SIM).
Display
6.5" IPS LCD, 720 × 1600 pixels (HD+), 400 nits typical.
Platform
Android 12, Realme UI S Edition. Unisoc Tiger T612 octa-core (12 nm). Mali-G57 GPU.
Memory
3GB + 32GB, 4GB + 64GB or 4GB + 128GB (UFS 2.2). Dedicated microSDXC slot.
Main Camera
50MP wide + 0.3MP depth. 1080p video at 30 fps.
Selfie Camera
5MP wide. 720p video at 30 fps.
Connectivity
Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 5.0, GPS. No NFC.
Ports and Sensors
micro-USB 2.0, 3.5mm headphone jack, loudspeaker, side-mounted fingerprint sensor.
Battery
5,000 mAh with 10W wired charging.

Specifications are from GSMArena. Memory options vary by country.
